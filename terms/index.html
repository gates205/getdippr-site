What Dippr is

Dippr reads API 5-in-1 aquarium test strips with your iPhone's camera and tells you which band each reading falls in, with a short piece of advice. It is a guide for keeping fish.

Not veterinary advice

Dippr is not a vet and does not replace one.

  • A test strip can only show a range, not an exact value. Dippr reports the band it can stand behind, and says when it can't tell.
  • A strip, a photo or the lighting can be wrong, and so can a reading.
  • Dippr's advice is general. It doesn't know which fish you keep or what else is going on in your tank.

If your fish seem unwell, or a reading worries you, check with a second test or a different kit, and ask an aquarium professional or a vet. You are responsible for the care of your animals.
