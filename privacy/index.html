Dippr Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Dippr is an iPhone app that reads aquarium test strips with your camera and tells you, in plain English, what your water needs. This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your readings and photos stay on your phone, and we don't track you.

What Dippr stores, and where

When you test a strip, Dippr saves:

  • the photo of your strip,
  • the colors it measured on each pad,
  • the date and time, and
  • the result it showed you.

It also keeps your progress (XP, streaks and Dippr's tankmates) and your settings.

All of this is stored on your iPhone only. Dippr has no accounts and no server of its own. We never receive your readings or photos.

Your camera

Dippr uses the camera only while you are on the Scan screen, to photograph your test strip. It uses the motion sensor at the same time, to help you hold the phone level and still. Nothing from the camera or the motion sensor leaves your phone.

Your photo library

In normal use, Dippr does not save anything to your photo library. It keeps its own copy of each strip photo inside the app.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Dippr schedules them on your phone. They are not sent from a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. Apple handles your payment details, and we never see them.

To check whether you have Premium, Dippr uses a service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription status, and basic technical details such as the app version and device type. It does not receive your name, email address, readings or photos. You can read RevenueCat's own privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your information.

Backups

Your Dippr data is included in your iPhone's normal backups (iCloud or your computer), the same as other apps. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Exporting and deleting your data

  • Export: Settings › Your data › Export my readings saves a copy of every reading as a spreadsheet file you can keep anywhere.
  • Delete: Settings › Your data › Delete all data removes every reading, photo, XP and tankmate from your phone. Deleting the app does the same.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Dippr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. As described above, Dippr does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
