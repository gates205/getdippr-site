Dippr Support

Questions, problems or ideas? We'd love to hear from you.

Email: support@getdippr.com

Every message is read, and answered as soon as possible.

What to include

  • What happened, and what you expected to happen.
  • Your iPhone model and the Dippr version (in Dippr: Settings › About › Version).
  • If a reading looks wrong: a photo of the strip, the lot number printed on the strip bottle, and the time you tested.
  • If you're happy to share them: your readings, from Settings › Your data › Export my readings.

Your readings stay on your phone. We only see what you choose to send us.

Common questions

  • Dippr says "Can't tell yet". That color falls between two bands, so Dippr won't guess. Test again with a fresh strip in a few days.
  • "I couldn't read this one clearly." Use a fresh strip on a dry spot of plain white printer paper, tap Start as you dip, and hold the phone flat and still.
  • Subscriptions. Manage or cancel in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

About Dippr's advice

Dippr gives general aquarium guidance from your test strip, in bands, with a short piece of advice. It is not veterinary advice. If your fish seem unwell, or a reading worries you, check with a second test and ask an aquarium professional or a vet.
